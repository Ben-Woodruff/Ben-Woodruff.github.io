Who am I?

©Photo taken by Benjamin Woodruff

Hello, and welcome to my website.

My name is Benjamin Woodruff, I'm a Mechatronics graduate currently studying the University of Manchester's MSc in robotics course. I'm part of RAINZ CDT as one of their first cohorts, and will be hopefully performing my responsibilities as a postgraduate researcher this coming year.

I chose research, and academia as a whole, because I enjoy the engineering process. I'm passionate about solving the big problems our society faces and using my skills to hopefully help people worldwide is my dream.

Research also allows me to teach others the same things I find interesting, while also continuing to learning myself and explore the forefronts of human knowledge

Research Interests

©Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 0.0 License. Source.

My current interests pertain to my research at the University of Manchester, namely sustainability, and how robotics and AI can help us achieve net zero carbon emissions . To this end, I'm currently researching automation within renewable energy, and how robots might be used in maintenance and inspection to help promote clean and reliable transfer and generation of power. Specifically, how we might improve autonomous maintenance robots with more intelligent designs and improve the quality of the data they recieve.

  • Rotary Double Inverted Pendulum (RDIP)

    Contribution: Sole contributor, wrote simulation & control.

I compared the outputs of the simulation to one solved using a set of differential equations that are iterativelty repeated. My project in particular looked at the dynamics when only the bottom angular encoder gave readings, which was unsuprisngly much harder than if you had access to all the system angles.
